Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 21, 2026

Welcome to Watchsave (“Watchsave,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Watchsave website and related services.

By creating an account or using Watchsave, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Watchsave, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password information in securely hashed form
  • Account creation date
  • Login and session information

We do not store your plain-text password.

Activity Information

  • Videos you watch or claim
  • Rewards earned
  • Account balance
  • Withdrawal requests
  • Withdrawal status
  • Dates and times of relevant activities
  • Messages sent through Watchsave private admin chat

Withdrawal Information

If you request a withdrawal, we may collect information necessary to process and review the request, including:

  • Bank or payment-provider name
  • Account number
  • Account holder name
  • Withdrawal amount
  • Withdrawal status

Technical Information

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate usage information
  • Login/session information
  • Security and error information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users
  • Keep users signed in
  • Provide Watchsave features
  • Track video activity and rewards
  • Maintain account balances
  • Process and review withdrawal requests
  • Communicate with users regarding withdrawals or account issues
  • Provide private support through admin chat
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Monitor platform security
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve the website
  • Maintain accurate records
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

3. Passwords and Account Security

Your Watchsave password is stored using password hashing rather than as plain text.

We also use authentication sessions and security controls to help protect accounts.

However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

4. Cookies and Login Sessions

Watchsave may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain secure login sessions and provide essential website functionality.

5. Advertising

Watchsave may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense, if those services are enabled on the website.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements according to their own policies and applicable laws.

6. Third-Party Services

Watchsave may use third-party services to operate certain parts of the platform.

Cloudinary

Watchsave may use Cloudinary to store and deliver videos uploaded by administrators.

PostgreSQL / Database Services

Watchsave stores account and platform information in a PostgreSQL database hosted through third-party infrastructure.

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information simply because you use Watchsave.

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate Watchsave, such as:

  • Hosting providers
  • Database providers
  • Cloud storage or media providers
  • Security providers
  • Advertising providers
  • Technical service providers

8. Admin Access

Authorized Watchsave administrators may have access to information necessary to operate and manage the platform, including account information, balances, withdrawal requests and private admin chats.

9. Private Admin Chat

Watchsave may provide a private chat between a user and Watchsave administrators, particularly in connection with withdrawal requests.

Messages sent through this feature may be stored in our database so that the conversation can be maintained and reviewed.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, process withdrawals, prevent fraud, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

11. Deleted Accounts

If an account is deleted or disabled, some information may remain in our systems for legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, financial records, backups, or legal compliance.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to certain personal information
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of certain information
  • Requesting information about how your information is used
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing where applicable
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent

13. Children's Privacy

Watchsave is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly request or intentionally collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

14. International Processing

Watchsave may use service providers whose systems or infrastructure are located in countries other than your country of residence.

15. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, database, server, or method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we may update the “Last Updated” date at the beginning of this policy.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact Watchsave through the official contact/support channel available on the website.
